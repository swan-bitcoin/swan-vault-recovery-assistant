Temporary Wallet Access

Swan Vault Recovery Assistant never saves any of your wallet information to your computer. This makes it a safe and reliable way to access your wallet, review transactions, and check your balance.

Enter your Wallet Configuration and click “Fetch Wallet”.

Multisig Recovery

Swan Vault Recovery Assistant can create and sign transactions to sweep your wallet to a safe location using your hardware signing devices even if your original wallet coordinator or service provider is not available.

Click “Start Recovery” after loading your wallet.

More Information

Swan Vault Recovery Assistant can load most descriptor wallets and assist with recovering common multisig wallets.

To load your wallet, Swan Vault Recovery Assistant uses an Electrum Server (Blockstream’s by default). If privacy is very important to you, select an Electrum Server which will protect your privacy (ideally your own).

Ensure that you have received Swan Vault Recovery Assistant from a trusted source. Ideally directly from the GitHub releases. If in doubt, the application itself is auditable, ask a trusted developer.
